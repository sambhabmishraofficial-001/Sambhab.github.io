Mini ramp

an evening session by the river

How to ride. Pump like a swing: hold (mouse, finger or space) on the way down, let go on the way up. Enough speed pops you over the coping. In the air, hold to grab the board, and let go before you land or you slam. Clean grabs raise the multiplier up to x5. You have 60 seconds.
