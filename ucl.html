A few facts about UCL

1826

Founded as London University, the first university in England to admit students regardless of their religion. Critics called it "the godless institution of Gower Street".

1878

The first university in England to admit women to degrees on equal terms with men.

10 columns

The Wilkins Building was designed by William Wilkins, architect of the National Gallery, and built between 1827 and 1829. Its portico of ten Corinthian columns sits beneath the famous dome.

Bentham

The philosopher Jeremy Bentham is seen as UCL's spiritual father. His "auto-icon", his preserved skeleton dressed in his own clothes, is on display in the Student Centre.

30+ Nobels

More than thirty Nobel Prize winners have studied or worked at UCL.

1904

John Ambrose Fleming invented the thermionic valve at UCL, the device that launched modern electronics.

Alumni

Mahatma Gandhi studied law here, Alexander Graham Bell and Francis Crick passed through, and Coldplay formed while its members were UCL students.

50,000+

Students from around 150 countries, on a campus in Bloomsbury in the heart of London. UCL is consistently ranked among the world's top ten universities.
